Rooftop Solar Pilot Report - Riverside Campus (2024)
1. Executive Summary
The pilot installed a 120 kW rooftop photovoltaic array on three campus buildings.
Installation was completed in March 2024 and the array was connected to the grid on 28 March 2024.
In its first twelve months the array generated 151 MWh of electricity.
This covered about 23 percent of the electricity used by the three buildings.
The pilot is considered successful and a second phase is recommended (see page 4).
=== page ===
2. System Design and Method
The array uses 300 monocrystalline panels rated at 400 W each.
Panels are mounted at a fixed tilt of 15 degrees and face south.
Four string inverters of 30 kW each convert the output to AC power.
A monitoring system records power, voltage and panel temperature every 15 minutes.
Generation data were compared with the utility meter readings every month.
=== page ===
3. Results
The annual capacity factor of the array was 14.4 percent.
The highest instantaneous output, 104 kW, was recorded at noon on 12 June 2024.
Total downtime was 2.1 percent of daylight hours.
Most downtime was caused by one inverter fault in August 2024, repaired within five days.
Panel cleaning twice a year kept soiling losses below 3 percent.
=== page ===
4. Costs and Recommendations
The total installed cost of the pilot was USD 168,000, including panels, inverters and labour.
Electricity bill savings in the first year were USD 21,500.
The simple payback period is therefore about 7.8 years.
Recommendation: in phase two, add 200 kWh of battery storage to shift midday generation to the evening peak.
Recommendation: extend the array to the library roof after its planned repair in 2026.
=== page ===
5. Lessons Learned
Structural surveys of the roofs took longer than planned and delayed installation by six weeks.
Early agreement with the facilities team on maintenance access avoided later disputes.
Students used the public monitoring dashboard in two energy courses during the autumn term.
Bird nesting under the panels on building C required protective mesh to be fitted in May.
